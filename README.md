# Elmina

**Try it here: https://sixseven-elmina-community.vercel.app/**

Elmina is a community app for people who live in City of Elmina. Neighbours meet through everyday activities, learn at monthly climate and wellness workshops, and keep track of their own recycling.

The idea in one line: **connection → awareness → action.**

## How to try it

1. Open the link above. It works on phone and computer.
2. Sign up as a new resident, or log in with a sample account:
   - Resident: `aina@example.com`
   - Workshop organiser: `drsiti@example.com`
   - Password for both: `elmina-demo`

   The login page also has one-tap buttons for these accounts.

If the first page is slow, give it a few seconds to wake up.

## Things to try

- **Home**: like and comment on neighbours' posts.
- **Activities**: join an activity with one tap, or host your own.
- **Workshops**: join this month's climate workshop. Log in as Dr Siti to host one.
- **Profile**: log your recycling and watch your monthly total go up, or share a photo from an activity (under 4 MB).

## Note

This is a prototype for the SDP University Hackathon 2026. All residents, posts and numbers are sample data. It is not an official Sime Darby Property service.

Built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and PostgreSQL.
